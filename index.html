The Challenge

End-of-life is a decision, not a checkbox

Operators have tracking and conjunction alerts. What they lack is a structured way to decide when a satellite should leave orbit — weighing remaining value against rising risk and a hard regulatory deadline.

It is an optimal-stopping problem with money on both sides: deorbit too early and you lose revenue; too late and you face breach, collision risk, liability and debris. And when nobody puts a price on waiting, the default is delay.

I
Engineering
Monitors fuel and subsystem health — but ignores economic value and regulatory timing constraints.
II
Compliance
Tracks static rules (25-year, now 5-year) without dynamic, stochastic risk — and the rules keep changing.
III
Finance
Models depreciation but lacks orbital context — and never prices what each month of delay costs.
Result: disconnected silos, no unified model, unpriced delay — and compounding liability.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Does ORSIVA control or command my satellite?
No. ORSIVA is advisory-only decision intelligence — it recommends when to act and shows the full working behind it. The operator always makes and executes the decision. It sits on top of your flight dynamics; it does not replace them.
What data do you need to score a satellite?
A public catalogue entry (TLE) is enough to start. Operators can optionally add real mass, cross-section and fuel to sharpen Δv budgets and decay estimates — the more you provide, the tighter the verdict.
How is the ORSIVA Score calculated?
It combines disposal risk and the economics of timing into a single 0–100 score — lower is safer, higher means act sooner — with every input logged and auditable. The full method is set out in a technical brief, available on request.
Is it aligned with regulation?
ORSIVA encodes the frameworks that govern end-of-life — the FCC 5-year rule, IADC guidelines, ESA debris-mitigation requirements and UK licensing conditions — as versioned rules. Changes are flagged by AI and approved by a human, and every verdict cites the rule and version it used. That supports your compliance planning; it is not a legal determination, which stays with you and your regulator.
What stage is ORSIVA at — can I pilot it?
The decision engine, fleet risk matrix, orbital console and backtest are live in preview now. We're selecting a small group of design-partner operators to run ORSIVA against real fleets in shadow mode. If that's you, start a conversation.
